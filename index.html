A memory of one city, 2,300 km away

“Big Ben” is a memory of a city: a student flat, the view from the window into a grey, misty morning and the chime of the great clock. That is where the idea was born — and the dream it started still goes on here, on the promenade in Tivat.

It began as a café of just 30 m² with seven tables in the garden. Today it is a restaurant and pizzeria with two terraces and 200 seats, known for quick, careful service.

Mediterranean dishes, wines of international quality and a light maestral breeze bringing the freshness of the sea.
